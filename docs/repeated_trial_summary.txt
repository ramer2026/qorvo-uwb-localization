Repeated-Trial Pilot Summary

Dataset:
- r1: original full 24-point dataset
- r2: independent repeated-trial subset
- r2 points:
  - z000_x000_y000
  - z050_x050_y050
  - z050_x050_y100
  - zm050_x000_y000
- Each r2 point has LOS and obstructed measurements from anchorA and anchorB.
- R2 completeness check: complete.

Evaluation:
- Train on r1 rows corresponding to the r2 points.
- Test on r2 repeated-placement rows.
- Features: range, RSSI, AoA, RSL/SNR diagnostics, diagnostic AoA.
- Model: ExtraTreesRegressor for 3D localization, RandomForestClassifier for LOS/obstructed condition.

Results:
- Training rows: 4,611
- Test rows: 4,637
- Test points: 4
- Condition accuracy: 65.2%
- Mean 3D error: 0.264 m
- Median 3D error: 0.189 m
- 75th percentile error: 0.339 m
- 90th percentile error: 0.687 m

Interpretation:
The repeated-trial pilot suggests that the localization model trained on the original dataset can transfer to independently re-placed tag/obstruction measurements for a small representative subset. Condition classification is less stable across repeated placements, motivating additional repeated-trial data and trial-robust calibration.
