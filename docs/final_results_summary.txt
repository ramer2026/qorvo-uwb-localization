Qorvo UWB LOS vs Obstruction 3D Localization Summary

Dataset:
- 24 matched 3D tag points
- 2 conditions: LOS and obstructed
- 2 anchors per condition
- 48 point-condition samples
- 27,931 clean per-measurement rows after filtering status != Ok and distance == 0

LOS vs Obstruction:
- RSSI significant in 48/48 point-anchor comparisons.
- path1 RSL significant in 48/48.
- path1 SNR significant in 48/48.
- peak SNR significant in 47/48.
- RSL mean significant in 47/48.
- diagnostic AoA type 0 significant in 46/48.
- printed AoA azimuth significant in 45/48.
- printed AoA elevation significant in 44/48.
- distance significant in 42/48.

Effect sizes:
- path1 RSL median |Cohen d| ≈ 3.94.
- path1 SNR median |Cohen d| ≈ 3.94.
- RSSI median |Cohen d| ≈ 3.51.
- RSL mean median |Cohen d| ≈ 4.85.
- distance median |Cohen d| ≈ 0.68.

Localization:
- Aggregate range + AoA baseline:
  mean 3D error = 0.696 m
  median 3D error = 0.683 m
- Aggregate range + AoA + signal/SNR:
  mean 3D error = 0.636 m
  median 3D error = 0.629 m
- Improvement over aggregate baseline:
  mean error reduction ≈ 0.060 m, about 8.6%
  median error reduction ≈ 0.053 m, about 7.8%

Obstruction classification:
- Aggregate distribution random forest:
  accuracy = 77.1%
  LOS recall = 87.5%
  obstructed recall = 66.7%

Main conclusion:
Obstruction strongly changes UWB diagnostic measurement distributions, especially RSSI/RSL/SNR. Distribution-level signal and SNR features improve 3D localization beyond range/AoA alone and provide moderate LOS/obstruction classification.
